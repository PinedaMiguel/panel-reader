Removed from the project (2026-10-10, archived outside the repo): *Blue Bolt* #5 p4 and *Jungle Comics* #16 p60 (single pages), and the *Y: The Last Man* excerpt (copyrighted). The library now uses only public-domain or permissively licensed comics.

Rejected: "Six-Gun Smith" (Wally Wood, *Western Crime Busters* #7, 1951): public domain, but page 2 shows a man tearing a woman's dress.
*America's Best Comics* #18 (Black Terror) — public domain, but WWII dialogue contains ethnic slurs the app would read aloud.
Avoid *Little Nemo* episodes featuring "Impie" (racist caricature, from 1906 on).
